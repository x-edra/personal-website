# welcome!

This website exists to collect thoughts, research, projects, ideas, and whatever else seems worth keeping.

Not merely for personal reference, but to document what I learn, explore what I don't understand, and share the knowledge gathered along the way.

Some entries may be technical, others speculative. Some ideas will grow into projects; others may remain unfinished notes. The goal isn't to have everything figured out, but to keep learning, questioning, building, and connecting ideas.

If something here proves useful to you, even better. Knowledge becomes more valuable when it can travel beyond the person who first acquired it.

Take a look around, follow whatever catches your attention, and feel free to come back whenever you like.




## how to explore

The archive is organized around three main sections, each serving a different purpose.

**01 / home — the central node**

The entry point to the archive. A starting place to navigate its contents, discover recent interests, and follow connections between ideas.

**02 / study — organizing knowledge**

A space for collecting, structuring, and connecting knowledge as clearly as possible. Notes, concepts, research, and references are organized to make information easier to understand, revisit, and build upon.

**03 / projects — from ideas to implementation**

A record of projects, experiments, and their development processes. Whenever legal, ethical, and practical constraints allow, the goal is to share reproducible procedures, technical details, documentation, and results so others can learn from or build upon the work.
